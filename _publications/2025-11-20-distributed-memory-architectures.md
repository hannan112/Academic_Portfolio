### Abstract
Modern supercomputing nodes combine multi-socket CPUs with multiple GPU accelerators linked through heterogeneous interconnects (PCIe, NVLink, and InfiniBand). This paper explores topology-aware collective scheduling algorithms that dynamically balance intra-node bandwidth bottlenecks and inter-node network saturation.
