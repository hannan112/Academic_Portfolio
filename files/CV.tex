	{Undergraduate Researcher --- Performance Evaluation of Execution Architectures}{Lahore, Pakistan}
	{COMSATS University Islamabad $\;\vert\;$ Advisor-supervised independent study}{2025 -- 2026}
	\cvlist
	\cvbullet{Investigated how execution strategy governs the throughput and scalability of a security-scanning workload, treating sequential, multithreaded, and multiprocess configurations as the independent variable in a controlled experiment.}
	\cvbullet{Designed the experimental methodology: repeated trials under fixed workloads, instrumented measurement of wall-clock latency, CPU utilization, resident memory, and throughput, and scaling sweeps over concurrency levels.}
	\cvbullet{Analyzed results with non-parametric hypothesis testing (Kruskal--Wallis, Mann--Whitney U) to establish statistical significance and to localize bottlenecks arising from process-level parallelism and I/O contention.}
	\cvbullet{Implemented a reusable benchmarking harness automating experiment orchestration, result aggregation, and statistical analysis, so that the evaluation is reproducible across workloads and machines.}
	\cvlistend

	{COMSATS University Islamabad $\;\vert\;$ Team lead, three-person team}{2025}
	\cvlist
	\cvbullet{Motivation: automated web security assessment is limited less by detection coverage than by false-positive alert triage, which displaces cost from detection to manual triage.}
	\cvbullet{Designed an end-to-end vulnerability analysis pipeline combining active scanning (OWASP ZAP, Nikto) with passive crawling behind a modular Django service interface.}
	\cvbullet{Developed a weakly supervised confidence-estimation approach (Random Forest) over scanner behavioral and contextual features, suppressing 29.5\% of alerts across 94,256 findings while preserving high-severity findings.}
	\cvbullet{Led experimental design, evaluation protocol, and written reporting for a three-person team.}
	\cvlistend
